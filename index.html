About

Short bio goes here. I design, build, and troubleshoot complex laser manufacturing and optical metrology systems—from beam delivery to process control. Currently a Ph.D. Candidate at UW–Madison (Industrial & Systems Engineering, Minor in Physics) with a physics foundation from The Ohio State University. My work bridges optical instrumentation with advanced manufacturing processes: · Laser Systems: Ultrafast and DUV beam profiling, delivery, precision alignment, and laser material interaction. · Optical Metrology: In-situ digital holographic microscopy (DHM), laser diffraction, high-speed microscopy and pyrometry. · Hardware & Process: Full-cycle integration (DFM/NPI), micro/metal additive manufacturing (EHD, DED, LPBF), and structured root cause problem-solving (RCA/DOE). · Tools: Zemax, COMSOL, SolidWorks, Python, OpenCV. Beyond the lab: When stepping away from the optics table, I’m usually in the garage doing mechanical tear-downs on project cars, exploring landscapes with a DSLR, or out on the trail. Whether tracking down an engine vibration or aligning a sub-micron laser spot, I enjoy the craft of understanding how complex physical systems work.
