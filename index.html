About

I design, build, and troubleshoot complex laser manufacturing and optical metrology systems—from beam delivery optics to closed-loop process control.

Currently a Ph.D. Candidate in Industrial & Systems Engineering (Minor in Physics) at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, with a physics background from The Ohio State University. My research bridges optical instrumentation with high-precision manufacturing:

  • Laser Systems: Ultrafast and DUV beam profiling, beam delivery, precision optical alignment, and laser-material interaction.
  • Optical Metrology: In-situ digital holographic microscopy (DHM), laser diffraction, high-speed imaging, and high-temperature pyrometry.
  • Hardware & Process: Full-cycle optomechanical integration (DFM/NPI), additive manufacturing (EHD, DED, LPBF), and root cause analysis (RCA/DOE).
